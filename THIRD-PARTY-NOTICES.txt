FFmpeg
------
The Windows executable contains an FFmpeg build, used to decode audio for the
fake-lossless / transcode checker. FFmpeg is free software licensed under the GNU
Lesser General Public License version 2.1 or later (some builds, under the GNU
General Public License version 2 or later). It is not part of this program and is
redistributed unmodified.

  Project : https://ffmpeg.org
  Licence : https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html
  Source  : https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg

The build is the one distributed by imageio-ffmpeg; its binaries and the
corresponding sources are published at:

  https://github.com/imageio/imageio-binaries

You do not have to use the bundled copy. Put your own `ffmpeg.exe` next to
`bpdl-web.exe`, or set the environment variable BPDL_FFMPEG to its full path, and
that one is used instead.
